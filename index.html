About FollowUp Pro

1

Creating a Topic

Click the New Topic button in the header. Give it a title and optional description. Topics are containers for your follow-up entries.

2

Adding Entries

Click on a topic to expand it, then use Add New Entry. Each entry needs a short title and a question you want to follow up on.

3

Follow-up Questions

Every entry has a Follow up button. Click it to add deeper nested questions, building a threaded conversation tree.

4

Completing & Reactivating

When you're done with a topic, click Complete Topic to archive it and earn XP. You can Reactivate a completed topic once to continue working on it.

5

XP & Level System

Earn XP by creating topics, adding entries, and completing them. Level up to unlock celebrations! The XP bar shows your progress to the next level.

6

Smart Ranking

Active topics are automatically ranked. Topics with more entries and recent activity appear higher on the list, so your most engaged topics stay at the top.

7

Search & Backup

Use the search bar to find entries across topics. Use Export and Import buttons to backup and restore your data.
